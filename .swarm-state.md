### Vitest 4 vs 2 decision

**Decision:** Pin vitest back to `^2.1.9` (the last green CI baseline).

**Rationale:** Vitest 4 internally imports `vite/module-runner`, which is only exported by Vite 6+. Keeping vitest 4 would therefore force a Vite 5 → 6 major migration, which is a broad, build-tool-level change beyond the scope of restoring the gate. The formant-vocoder PR did not appear to require vitest-4-specific APIs (no `@fast-check/vitest` imports in source), so the safest path is to restore the previous toolchain and schedule a deliberate vitest/vite upgrade separately.
