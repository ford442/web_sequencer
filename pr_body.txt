💡 What: Replaced array iterators (.find, .forEach) with standard for loops in HardwareModule.tsx. 🎯 Why: These closures execute frequently via requestAnimationFrame, causing GC pressure and potential micro-stutters during intensive rendering. 📊 Impact: Removes array iteration overhead and closure allocations from the main thread UI ticking logic. 🔬 Measurement: Verify stable frame rates during hardware rendering and absence of frequent micro-pauses in JS profiles.
