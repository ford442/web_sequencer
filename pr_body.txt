## 💡 What
Replaced the plain text "This plugin declares no automatable parameters" message in `Wam2GenericEditor` with the application's standardized empty state pattern.

## 🎯 Why
When users explore WAM plugins that happen to not declare any host-automatable parameters, presenting a bare line of grey text feels broken or unfinished compared to the rest of the application. Applying the standard dashed-border empty-state pattern gives this structural weight and clarifies that the lack of parameters is an expected state.

## 📸 Before/After
*Before:* Plain `<p className="text-xs text-gray-400">` text without structural framing.
*After:* A dashed-border container (`border-dashed`), a centered icon inside a circular background, and a clear title/subtitle hierarchy (`h3` for title, `p` for description).

## ♿ Accessibility
The SVG icon is strictly decorative and marked with `aria-hidden="true"`. The title and description use standard semantic tags.
